### Detailed Breakdown of Citations (Ranked by Frequency)

#### 1\. OISD-STD-174 Cl. 6.8 (V) / Cl. 6.8 (Well Control / Drilling & Workover Operations)

* **Total References:** **6 mentions** (4 in case reports, 2 in summaries) 1-3, 19, 20\.  
* **Case IDs:** Case 5 (E\&P/13) 1, 19 and Case 9 (E\&P/21) 2, 20\.  
* **Violation Description:** Operating without an active primary barrier. In **Case 5**, the trip tank was not connected during perforation and its level alarm was defunct 1\. In **Case 9**, the trip tank was kept completely isolated during perforation 2\. Both cases recommended that trip tanks must always be connected and operational for proper well monitoring as mandated by this clause 19, 20\.

#### 2\. OISD-STD-176 (including Table-1) (Safety Training and Competency)

* **Total References:** **5 mentions** (4 in case reports, 1 in summaries) 4-6, 21-23.  
* **Case IDs:** Case 2 (E\&P/05) 21, 22, Case 5 (E\&P/13) 4, 23, and Case 7 (E\&P/18) 5\.  
* **Violation Description:** Failure to train personnel or write training requirements into contracts. In **Case 2**, height-safety training under this standard was missing for the newly approved topman 21, 22\. In **Case 5**, essential training was not provided to the crew nor written into the contract 4, 23\. In **Case 7**, contract terms failed to mandate the critical safety courses defined in **Table-1** of OISD-STD-176 5\.

#### 3\. OISD-STD-174 Cl. 6.3.1 (B) / Cl. 6.3.1 (BOP Stack Configuration Requirements)

* **Total References:** **6 mentions** (3 in case reports, 3 in summaries) 7-9.  
* **Case IDs:** Case 5 (E\&P/13) 7 and Case 9 (E\&P/21) 8\.  
* **Violation Description:** Performing perforations in gas-bearing zones without a **Blind Shear Ram (BSR)** in the blowout preventer (BOP) stack 7, 8\. Planners assumed the formations were solely oil-bearing based on GTOs and ignored historical and drilling gas shows, leading to an inability to shear wirelines and seal the well when gas blowouts occurred 7-9.

#### 4\. OISD-STD-188 Cl. 5.10 (Pipeline Corrosion Control)

* **Total References:** **3 mentions** (2 in Case 10, 1 in summaries) 10, 11\.  
* **Case ID:** Case 10 (PL/03).  
* **Violation Description:** Underdosing corrosion inhibitor. The operator failed to maintain an adequate Corrosion Inhibitor (CI) dosing rate, violating **Clause 5.10**, which requires pipeline corrosion rates to be kept strictly below **1 MPY (mils per year)** 10, 11\.

#### 5\. OISD/PL/GC/01 Cl. 2.d (Guidelines for Preventing Pipeline Failure Due to Heavy Rainfall/Floods)

* **Total References:** **2 mentions** 12, 13\.  
* **Case ID:** Case 12 (PL/16).  
* **Violation Description:** Failing to proactively protect river-crossing pipeline sections near active meandering locations 12\. The guidelines, circulated on **15th October 2025**, require operators to identify highly vulnerable erosion zones and implement protective measures such as gabion walls, bed/bank protection, or longer horizontal directional drilling (HDD) realignments 12, 24, 25\.

#### 6\. OISD-STD-174 Cl. 4.1 (Workover well planning)

* **Total References:** **1 mention** 14\.  
* **Case ID:** Case 5 (E\&P/13).  
* **Violation Description:** Formulation of a workover plan without reviewing adjacent historical well records 14\. The well planners ignored offset well testing and previous zone transfer data that showed active gas flows, incorrectly designing the workover plan for an oil well instead of a gas well 7, 14\.

#### 7\. OISD-STD-105 (Permit-to-Work Systems)

* **Total References:** **1 mention** 15\.  
* **Case ID:** Case 11 (PL/07).  
* **Violation Description:** Severe gaps in contract management and execution. Work permits were issued without recording the contractor’s authorized supervisor’s name, and mechanized excavation was performed without informing the pipeline owner, resulting in a fatal trench cave-in 15, 26, 27\.

#### 8\. OISD-STD-139 (Offshore Pipeline Inspection)

* **Total References:** **1 mention** 11\.  
* **Case ID:** Case 10 (PL/03).  
* **Violation Description:** Operating a pipeline since commissioning without carrying out mandatory inline or pigging integrity inspections, allowing severe subsea anomalies to remain undetected 11, 28\.

#### 9\. OISD-STD-189 (Rig Firefighting System Standards)

* **Total References:** **1 mention** 16\.  
* **Case ID:** Case 9 (E\&P/21).  
* **Violation Description:** Operating a workover rig with critical firefighting equipment completely missing, notably a dedicated site fire water pump, violating emergency preparedness standards 2, 16\.

#### 10\. OISD-GDN-206 Cl. 7.5.1 (j)

* **Total References:** **1 mention** 17\.  
* **Case ID:** Case 2 (E\&P/05).  
* **Violation Description:** Lack of structured, regular safety inspections of company and contractor tools, tackles, and rig equipment by a competent person 17\.

#### 11\. OISD-GDN-182 Cl. 5.2.5 (A, iii)

* **Total References:** **1 mention** 18\.  
* **Case ID:** Case 2 (E\&P/05).  
* **Violation Description:** Lapses in elevated climbing safety 18\. The topman failed to anchor his full-body harness double hooks to the monkey board ladder during his ascent, relying solely on a fall prevention device (FPD) that subsequently failed 18, 29\.

### Related Regulatory Standards Cited

* **Working Group Committee Report Cl. 7.2 & 7.3.2:** Cited in **Case 1 (E\&P/04)** 32, 33 and **Case 11 (PL/07)** 15\. This report mandates induction/orientation transitions and safety performance ratings for contractors 15, 32, 33\.
