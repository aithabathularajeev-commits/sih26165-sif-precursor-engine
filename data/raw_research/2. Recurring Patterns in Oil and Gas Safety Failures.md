The safety case studies in your sources reveal several critical recurring patterns where unsafe conditions and procedural lapses occurred in more than one incident. Six of the most prominent repeating patterns, along with their associated case IDs, exact source phrasings, and the safety outcomes, are detailed below.

### 1\. Omission of a Blind Shear Ram (BSR) from the Blowout Preventer (BOP) Stack

This lapse involves performing high-risk well perforation activities without including a Blind Shear Ram in the BOP stack, primarily because planners underestimated gas risks and assumed the formations were solely oil-bearing 1-4.

* **Case IDs:**  
* **Case 5 (OISD/CS/2025-26/E\&P/13):** Blowout after well perforation 5\.  
* **Case 9 (OISD/CS/2025-26/E\&P/21):** Gas blowout incident at Workover Rig 5\.  
* **Exact Phrasing in the Sources:**  
* **Case 5:** *"As per Clause 6.3.1 (B) of OISD-STD-174, for gas wells with surface pressures ranging from 3000 psi to 5000 psi, the BOP stack shall include a Blind Shear Ram. However, in this case, the well was considered an oil well based on the approved well plan hence a Blind Shear Ram was not included in the BOP stack configuration."* 1, 2  
* **Case 9:** *"BOP stack had no shear ram, violating Clause 6.3.1, making it impossible to shear wireline and seal the well during gas flow."* 6 and *"BOP stack lacked a blind shear ram (BSR) which is violation of Cl.6.3.1 of OISD-STD-174."* 7  
* **Safety Outcome:**  
* **Case 5:** **Near-miss / Incident** (*"There was no fire or injury reported due to the incident."*) 8  
* **Case 9:** **Near-miss / Incident** (*"No injuries or fire were reported."*) 3

### 2\. Operating with an Isolated or Defunct Trip Tank

A breakdown in primary well barriers occurred when crews operated without using a trip tank or ignored defective trip tank alarm systems during perforation 6, 9\.

* **Case IDs:**  
* **Case 5 (OISD/CS/2025-26/E\&P/13)** 5  
* **Case 9 (OISD/CS/2025-26/E\&P/21)** 5  
* **Exact Phrasing in the Sources:**  
* **Case 5:** *"The trip tank was not in use during the incident, and the level alarm system of the trip tank was also defunct at the time of incident. This is violation of clause 6.8 (V) of OISD-STD-174."* 9  
* **Case 9:** *"The trip tank was kept isolated during perforation, which violated Clause 6.8(V) of OISD-STD-174."* 6  
* **Safety Outcome:**  
* **Case 5:** **Near-miss / Incident** 8  
* **Case 9:** **Near-miss / Incident** 3

### 3\. Exceeding Recommended Trip Speeds (Swabbing-Induced Influx)

Crews ran or pulled perforation tool strings faster than SOP-recommended limits, inducing a suction (swabbing) effect that triggered gas influx from the formation 4, 9\.

* **Case IDs:**  
* **Case 5 (OISD/CS/2025-26/E\&P/13)** 5  
* **Case 9 (OISD/CS/2025-26/E\&P/21)** 5  
* **Exact Phrasing in the Sources:**  
* **Case 5:** *"Analysis of well logging data indicated that the perforation gun was run in at an average speed which exceeded the recommended RIH speed specified in the SOP... the possibility of swabbing-induced influx could not be ruled out as a contributing factor."* 9, 10  
* **Case 9:** *"During wireline tool recovery, pull out was done too quickly, which may have further contributed by swabbing effect and situation worsened."* 4  
* **Safety Outcome:**  
* **Case 5:** **Near-miss / Incident** 8  
* **Case 9:** **Near-miss / Incident** 3

### 4\. Complete Absence of Supervision During Critical or High-Risk Activities

Supervisory overwatch completely broke down during hazardous, non-routine activities. This manifested as failing to assign mentors to inexperienced staff, utilizing unsupervised "unskilled" crews, or supervisors being entirely absent from the site 11-14.

* **Case IDs:**  
* **Case 2 (OISD/CS/2025-26/E\&P/05):** Fatal Accident at Workover Rig (Monkey board fall) 5\.  
* **Case 4 (OISD/CS/2026-27/E\&P/09):** Major incident at a GGS 5\.  
* **Case 8 (OISD/CS/2025-26/E\&P/19):** Fatal Accident during dismantling of Drilling Rig 5\.  
* **Case 11 (OISD/CS/2024-25/PL/07):** Fatal incident in Pipeline ROW during excavation 5\.  
* **Exact Phrasing in the Sources:**  
* **Case 2:** *"No mentor or supervisor was assigned to provide on-the-job guidance, leaving the victim potentially unaware of essential safety procedures and associated hazards."* 11  
* **Case 4:** *"All the workers involved in the job were in the "unskilled" category. No supervision was available."* 12  
* **Case 8:** *"Installation Manager (IM \- Company), HSE Engineer (Company), Rig Superintendent (Contractual), Rig Manager (Contractual) and HSE Advisor (Contractual)- All these key personnel were absent at the same time from the worksite during the critical job. Hence, job was done without adequate supervision."* 13  
* **Case 11:** *"Incident happened because an unsupervised activity was being carried out without considering & mitigating all probable hazards, like excavation up to 3.7 meter..."* 14, 15  
* **Safety Outcome:**  
* **Case 2:** **Fatal** (*"One fatality"* of a recently approved topman) 11, 16  
* **Case 4:** **Major** (*"Loss of vision of a Contractual worker"* after trapped pressure released from a flange) 17, 18  
* **Case 8:** **Fatal** (*"Fatal Accident"* of a floorhand crushed during block movement) 19-21  
* **Case 11:** **Fatal** (*"One Fatality"* due to a trench cave-in) 22, 23

### 5\. Team Communication Breakdowns on Rig Floors

Critical operational status details were not communicated between separate crew components (e.g., floor crew to elevated topman, or crane operator to signalman), leading to premature actions or unexpected equipment movements 24, 25\.

* **Case IDs:**  
* **Case 6 (OISD/CS/2025-26/E\&P/14):** Fatal accident caused by tubing drop 5\.  
* **Case 8 (OISD/CS/2025-26/E\&P/19):** Fatal Accident during dismantling of Drilling Rig 5\.  
* **Exact Phrasing in the Sources:**  
* **Case 6:** *"During the incident, the information regarding the placement of the pin end on the incline ramp at the V-door was not conveyed to the topman. The topman was unaware of the activity taking place on the rig floor... the necessary information... did not get communicated to him."* 24 and *"The topman unlatched the tubing double from the elevator while the pin end was on the incline ramp due to communication gap."* 26  
* **Case 8:** *"Communication gap was observed between signalman and Crane operator. Signalman informed that signal was given to provide load to the sling which was attached in Crane hook while the crane operator claimed that no such signal was given."* 25  
* **Safety Outcome:**  
* **Case 6:** **Fatal** (*"Fatality of the Night Tool Pusher operating the driller console, Critical injury to a Rig-man"*) 27  
* **Case 8:** **Fatal** (*"Fatal Accident"* of a floorhand) 19, 21

### 6\. Administrative and Execution Delays for Known Corrective Risks

Engineers identified critical vulnerabilities and initiated permanent corrective plans (such as horizontal directional drilling realignments or safe pipeline pressure limits), but management failed to implement immediate temporary mitigations or expedite repairs before operational failure occurred 28, 29\.

* **Case IDs:**  
* **Case 10 (OISD/CS/2025-26/PL/03):** Oil Pipeline leakage Incident in an Offshore Pipeline 5\.  
* **Case 12 (OISD/CS/2025-26/PL/16):** Pipeline failure incidents due to heavy rainfall/flash floods 5\.  
* **Exact Phrasing in the Sources:**  
* **Case 10:** *"Pipeline was continued to be operated above the safe operating pressure for more than one year after latest MTM survey report of December-2023. Immediate measures like bringing down the pressure in initial 40 KM section below safe pressure or reviewing Risk-1 anomalies for any suitable mitigation were not taken."* 28  
* **Case 12:** *"In some of the incidents, although reiver bank erosion was noticed and fresh HDD of longer length/realignment were planned, delay in execution had resulted in failure of pipeline during operation."* 29  
* **Safety Outcome:**  
* **Case 10:** **Major** (*"Loss of crude oil"* / *"loss of crude as per difference between pumped and receipt quantity was 382 KL"*) 30, 31  
* **Case 12:** **Major** (*"Pipeline failure... Loss of petroleum product/gas due to snapping of pipelines. Additionally, pipeline replacement costs and throughput loss..."*) 32
